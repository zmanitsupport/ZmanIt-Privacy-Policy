ZmanIt Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the ZmanIt Android app ("ZmanIt", "the app") collects, uses, shares and deletes your information. ZmanIt is provided by Smoovo INC., New York, United States ("we", "us"). By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect
Account information: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google account name and email. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
Location: the app requires access to your device's GPS location (precise location) to calculate prayer, Shabbat and holiday times for where you are. Location is collected only while the app is open and only when you tap the button to get your location. We do not track your location in the background. Your coordinates are stored in your profile so times can be shown. If you deny location permission, the app cannot calculate times for your location.
Device identifier: a unique device identifier (such as the device serial number or Android ID) and device model, used only to enforce the free trial (one trial per device).
Content you create: custom calendar events, reminders and birth information you enter in the app.
Trial and subscription status: trial start/end dates and whether your subscription is active. Payments are processed by Google Play; we do not receive or store your card or payment details.
App settings (language, theme, time format, notification choices) are stored on your device.
2. How we use information
Calculate and display Halachic times, Shabbat times, the Hebrew calendar and holidays.
Create and manage your account, free trial and subscription access.
Send notifications you enable (scheduled locally on your device).
Prevent trial abuse and keep the service secure.
Provide customer support.
We access stored user data only for these purposes (support, security, abuse prevention and maintaining the service). We do not use your data for advertising and do not sell your personal information.

3. Free trial and subscription
ZmanIt includes a 30-day free trial. When the trial ends, your subscription automatically begins at $2 per month, billed yearly at $24, through Google Play, unless you cancel before the trial ends. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in Google Play (Play Store > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions). Billing, refunds and payment data are handled by Google under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Sharing
We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share data only with:

Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which host our accounts and database;
Google Play (billing and subscription management) and Google Sign-In if you choose it;
Authorities, when required by law or a valid legal request, or to protect our rights, users or security.
The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

5. Data retention and deletion
We keep your account data while your account is active. You can delete your account and data at any time:

In the app: Settings > Account Management > Delete Account.
By email: send a deletion request to contact@smoovo.com from the email address on your account (subject: "ZmanIt account deletion"). We process requests within 30 days.
Deletion removes your account, email, profile, saved location, custom events, reminders and birth information, and trial/subscription records from our systems. The only data we retain is the device identifier, flagged as belonging to a former user, so that the free trial cannot be used repeatedly on the same device. It is not linked to your email or any other personal information. Your Google Play subscription is separate; cancel it in Google Play.

6. Security
Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase, which applies industry-standard security. No method is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Your rights
Depending on where you live (including the EU/UK under GDPR and California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to processing. Email contact@smoovo.com to exercise these rights. Our legal bases under GDPR are performing our contract with you (providing the app) and our legitimate interest in preventing trial abuse. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. International transfers
We serve users worldwide. Your data may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates, which may have different data protection laws than your country.

9. Children
ZmanIt is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version, and material changes will be communicated in the app or by email.
